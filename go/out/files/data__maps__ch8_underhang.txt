11	A breach in the hull. Through it: the underside of the world, and wind, and a long way down.
12	The captain's shelf. Hymnals, a logbook, and a child's drawing of the sun, pinned up carefully. The sun has a face. It is smiling. It is not moving.
13	Gloam crystals grown through the deck like teeth. They hum in the same key as the lullaby.
14	The lullaby swells, sweet and endless, from the bow. Somewhere in this ship, children are sleeping. Find them first.
