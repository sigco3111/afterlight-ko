Wreck
A Choir supply barge, sunk with its cargo: thousands of candles, still in their wrappers. Every one unlit.
An old Aurenne galleon, two hundred years down. The figurehead is a woman holding a lantern. Someone, long ago, gave her a face like a mother's.
A smuggler's sloop. In the hold, crates stamped PROPERTY OF C. ASHE — DO NOT OPEN. Corvin pretends not to see them.
Ledger
A cot, made with hospital corners. A child's drawing is tucked under the pillow: a big woman in a hood, and the words MOTHER OSK MAKES IT STOP HURTING.
A cot, made with hospital corners. On the blanket, a single dried flower, placed very carefully.
Vigil stone
A cairn of gray stones. On the largest, chiseled by someone who was not a mason: SIR ALDOUS VENN. HE STOOD BETWEEN.
A stone knight, sword point-down, head bowed. The plinth reads: DAME ORSOLA OF THE WESTMARCH. SHE WAS AFRAID OF HORSES.
A stone knight with a cracked shield. The plinth reads: SIR TEODOR BRAND. HE WEPT AT EVERY KNIGHTING, INCLUDING HIS OWN.
A stone knight, very young, very serious. The plinth reads: SIR ALDOUS VENN, AGE NINETEEN. Somebody has scratched underneath, much later: STILL AN IDIOT. — G.T.
The Rook Queen's nest: a ring of buttons, spoons, bottle caps, a brass doorknob, three wedding rings and, right in the middle, a child-sized empty space shaped like a knife.
A kite with a lopsided sun painted on it, tangled in the junk. A name on the spar in crayon: TILLY. PROPERTY OF. KEEP OFF.
Hal's cabin
The Kingfisher's mainmast, snapped. Her colors still fly from the stump: Dawnguard blue, with a kingfisher stitched over the sunburst by someone who could not sew.
A bow gun, still loaded. The gunner's gloves are on the breech, laid down neatly side by side, as if he meant to come back for them.
A small bed with a patchwork quilt. Every patch is a different shade of yellow.
A shelf of wooden animals: a gull, a cat, a lopsided horse. Carved by a twelve-year-old, badly, with enormous care.
Telescope
A star chart, inked on sailcloth. Half the stars are crossed out. Beside the gaps, in tiny writing: "fell?" "fell." "FELL. Bother."
A copy of The Sunward Atlas, annotated in red ink by its own author. The margins are fuller than the pages.
A shelf of books about the sun. Every one ends with the same chapter title: "And Then It Rose Again."
