Look west
The bow cuts through cloud the color of a bruise. Below: nothing at all, for a very long way.
The mainmast. Someone has carved PIP into it at a height that suggests standing on a barrel. Below it, smaller: CORVIN SAYS NO.
The foremast. A patched sail, a patched patch, and a single kite tangled in the rigging.

Forge
Corvin's chart table. Under the charts, a small painting of a woman in a Dawnguard sky-wing coat, laughing at whoever held the brush. On the back: "H.M. — don't you DARE frame this." It is framed.
Corvin's books: "Navigation for the Reluctant," "Debts and How to Outlive Them," and a much-read book of children's bedtime stories with the price still on it.
Ilse's stove. Something is simmering. It smells like home, if home were mostly onions.
Ilse's shelf: bandages rolled tight, tinctures labeled in tiny exact handwriting. One jar just says: PIP. NO.
Nell's forge, built into the ship's boiler. It runs on coal, spite and, lately, the heat of a Sun Shard on a long chain.
