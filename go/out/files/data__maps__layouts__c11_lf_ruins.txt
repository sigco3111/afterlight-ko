The door is fused shut with Gloam-glass. Through the window: a chair, pushed back from a table, exactly as someone left it.
The Gull's Rest. The sign still swings. There is no wind.
A wall of light fills the doorway, warm as a hand. It will not let you in. Not while the island is still remembering so loudly.
