1	Lemonade (with mint)
2	Tomas's lemonade, for Bess. It has been on its way for forty years.
3	Shardling
4	A splinter of the broken sun that learned to walk. It is warm, and it is not kind.
5	Noonhound
6	The Choir's hounds, bred in the Spire's light. They cast no shadow at all.
7	Glare
8	An eye of noon, floating. Looking at it is like looking at the sun, which nobody has done for a year.
9	Vesper Lancer
10	The Spire's guard. They have been promised the best day of their lives, as soon as their shift ends.
11	Choir Seraph
12	A hymn given wings. It heals whatever stands between it and you.
13	Keeper of the Spire's back door. He has turned away four hundred pilgrims and remembers every face.
14	Hour Knight
15	Armor wound like a clock. It stops when it is struck — and so, for a moment, do you.
16	Stillwisp
17	A moment that never ended, drifting. Cold as an afternoon that will not let you go home.
18	Memory Moth
19	They eat memories the way other moths eat coats. The Spire is a feast.
20	They practice the Vespersong in shifts. Somebody, somewhere in the Spire, is always singing it.
21	The Hour-Warden
22	The Spire's keeper of hours. It has only ever counted to twelve, and it has done so for forty years.
23	Festival Puppet
24	"Lovely day for it!" it says, and smiles, and smiles, and comes for your throat.
25	Paper Lantern
26	A festival lantern that drifted loose. The candle inside has burned for forty years and never got any shorter.
27	Gilded Hound
28	Somebody's dog, remembered as a very good dog. Remembered a little too hard.
29	"Welcome to the Festival of the Long Light. Enjoy the day." Forever.
30	Maypole Dancer
31	She has been dancing round the maypole since before your parents were born. She would love you to join in.
32	Choir of the Dead
33	Mercy's congregation. They sing for her because she promised them they would never have to stop.
34	Evernoon's warden. She believes that a day which never ends cannot hurt anyone. She has never once been told no and believed it.
35	Unravelling
36	Where Evernoon comes apart, the Gloam comes in. It wears whatever it finds.
37	Frayed Puppet
38	Its strings are cut. Its sentence is unfinished. It is very, very angry about both.
39	What the Noon Forgot
40	Every sentence Evernoon never finished, knotted together into something that wants, very badly, to be heard.

64	Mercy lets down her hood. "You will not take her. You will not take ANY of them." She sings the Vespersong.
65	A single soft bell begins to toll, counting someone down...
66	"Hush. Be still. Be still forever."
67	"Rest now." A lullaby — and under it, the bells of Evernoon begin to swing...
68	"—IT!" A hundred mouths finish the sentence at once.
69	It begins to forget its own shape. Light pours through the gaps — light no longer heals it!
70	A puppet tears loose from its body and staggers forward!
