1	Charcoal drawings on the bell tower wall: four stick-children wearing crowns. Under them, in careful letters: *THE GUTTER CORT · PIP · NELL · TUCK · WICK.* Two of the crowns have been rubbed out and drawn back in, darker.
2	...Don't look at that. That's private property. Of the Court.
3	From up here you can see the whole City of Bells: the Cathedral, the Grand Lantern, and far off to the south, the brown thread of the Pilgrim Road.
4	No time — he's getting away!
5	The bells will ring at noon! And at noon tomorrow! And the noon after that!
6	What a summer, eh? Feels like it'll never end.
7	Lysa! Your brother's looking for you. He's _always_ looking for you.
8	Hush — the Keeper's going up. Watch the gallery. It's the best part.
