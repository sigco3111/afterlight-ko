1	Ward Water
2	A bucket from the rationed well, for the Ember Ward.
3	Sunsalt Phials
4	The Choir's remedy for Gloam-rot. Each phial glows like a coal kept under ash.
5	Three Tin Tags
6	DORAN. MAGS. LITTLE COBB. The third sunsalt team.
7	Captain Buttons
8	A rag doll with one button eye. Very brave.
9	Dawnmoss Seeds
10	Gold-green spores in a Choir apothecary tin. They will grow in anything.
11	Bell Clapper
12	The clapper of the Bellwrights' Tenor. Heavy as a promise, dented from the fall.
13	Dead Letters
14	Three letters from Petra's bag, posted the day before the world ended.
15	A Letter for Wren
16	"My dear restless heart — I have kept a place for you." Choir seal, broken.
17	Rotgnawer
18	Undercroft rats that ate what the rot left behind, and caught it. Their bite carries it on.
19	Rot Bloom
20	Gloam-rot, given time and darkness and nothing else to do, learns to flower.
21	Drowned Acolyte
22	An acolyte of the Under-Choir. It drowned singing. It is still singing.
23	Knell Wisp
24	The last note of a bell that broke mid-toll. It drifts, still ringing, looking for the rest of itself.
25	Ossuary Warden
26	Aurenne's honored dead were buried with their swords. Some of them never put the swords down.
27	The Unfinished
28	One of Sister Mercy's Remembrances, never finished. It is trying very hard to remember a face. Any face.
29	Cistern Eel
30	They came up the drains when the sea fell into the sky. They are not going back.
31	Bellshell Crab
32	It wears a handbell for a shell. When it is angry, which is always, it rings.
33	Drowned Cantor
34	A cantor of the Under-Choir. Its voice holds the others together, the way a nail holds a coffin.
35	Drowned Chorister
36	One voice in the Drowned Choir. Alone, it is only a drowned child. Together, they are a hymn.
37	The Under-Choir's Precentor. He was told to hold the note until noon. He is a very obedient man.
38	Old Clapper
39	A rust-red wyrm that nested in the Reliquary and swallowed a bell whole. It tolls when it breathes.
40	The Held Note breaks over you — the Dawnsong, sung backward!
41	The harmony falters. The Precentor's note cracks into a wail.
42	"Louder. LOUDER. Let the water carry it!" The cistern surges.
43	"Hold it... hold it until noon..."
44	The Precentor lifts his baton. A drowned voice answers from the water.
45	The choristers hold one swelling note. Silence one of them — quickly!
46	_BONNNG._ The sound comes out as fire.
47	Old Clapper thrashes. Rust and bell-metal rain from the ceiling!
48	Deep in Old Clapper's belly, something begins to toll. Brace yourselves!
49	The City of Bells, silent and drifting. Ilse's Ember Ward keeps its people breathing.
