The Held Note breaks over you — the Dawnsong, sung backward!
The harmony falters. The Precentor's note cracks into a wail.
"Louder. LOUDER. Let the water carry it!" The cistern surges.
"Hold it... hold it until noon..."
The Precentor lifts his baton. A drowned voice answers from the water.
The choristers hold one swelling note. Silence one of them — quickly!
_BONNNG._ The sound comes out as fire.
Old Clapper thrashes. Rust and bell-metal rain from the ceiling!
Deep in Old Clapper's belly, something begins to toll. Brace yourselves!
