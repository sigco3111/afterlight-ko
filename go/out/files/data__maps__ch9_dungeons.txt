Winch
The Marigold. A sky-ferry. Forty seats, still bolted in, all facing the windows.
On one seat, a paper bag of sweets, unopened. Someone was saving them for the view.
You climb the crane-tower. From the top, the Boneyard is a river of broken ships, running all the way down the canyon to the dark.
Far to the north-east, three bright shards hang in a slanting line, as if someone had thrown them and they never landed.
Strongbox
The strongbox lies open and quiet. Whatever it was, it is only a box now.
A gilded strongbox, half-buried beside the upturned hull of the Gilded Gull. The lock is shaped like a smile.
Pry it open?
The smile gets wider.
The Gilded Gull, lying on her back like a beetle. Her lift-coil housing is empty, torn open from the inside.
Censer
Luggage, still strapped to the rail. A name-tag: TILDE, AGED 9, TO AURENNE. PLEASE RETURN IF LOST.
Nobody did.
A barrel of hymnals, swollen with rain into a single block of paper. The top one is open to "Rest Ye, Dawn-Bound."
A ward of crystallized light hums around the mainmast. Up close you can hear it: the hymn, over and over, the same eight bars.

Crow's nest
The crow's nest has snapped off and rolled to this islet. You climb into it. From up here, through the rain, the shards wheel slowly around a single point of light.
The point of light does not wheel. It sits in the middle of everything, perfectly still.

Shelf
The captain's bunk, made with corners you could cut bread with. Under the pillow: a pressed flower and a list of hymns, with "Rest Ye" underlined twice.
Valve
A crack in the rock runs all the way up through the shard. At the top, impossibly far, a sliver of sky.
Through it, every so often, the shards drift past in a slow procession — and in the middle of them all, one white point that never moves at all.
An ember crystal as tall as Bram. Inside it, very small, a shape like a bird with its wings spread, caught mid-flight.
