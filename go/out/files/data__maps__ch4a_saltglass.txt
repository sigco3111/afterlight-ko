2	Prices are fixed. Fixed by me. Daily. Sometimes hourly.
3	Caravans
4	Teo hasn't stopped sulking since your little friend came out of the Lantern glowing.
5	Thirty days until his birthday. I'm going to need a bigger sulk-bucket.
6	We walked from Thistledown so my Teo could be Kindled in Saltglass light. His grandfather was. And his grandfather.
7	Now we wait thirty days for his birthday, in a town where water costs more than wine.
8	We're playing Lanterns and Gloam! I'm the Gloam! RAAAH!
9	Rafi is ALWAYS the Gloam. He says the Gloam is the best part.
10	The Gloam is NOT the best part. The best part is when the sun comes back.
11	The children sing on the Lantern steps every morning. The Keeper sits with them.
12	She says it's the worst choir she's ever heard. She cried the first time.
13	Yrsa looks up at you and hums three notes of the morning hymn. Then she goes back to drawing a bell in the sand.
14	Well
15	The deep well of Saltglass. The rope goes down, and down, and down.
16	You drop a pebble. Four heartbeats later, very faintly: _plink._
17	Chimes
18	Master Ruel's furnace. The air above it wobbles like a mirage that can't make up its mind.
19	The Lamp
20	The lamp of Saltglass burns inside a cage of saltglass panes. The light comes out of the glass in every direction at once.
21	Up close it hums — low, like a cat that's pleased with itself. Or a cat that isn't.
22	A mirror of polished saltglass. Your reflection is a half-step behind you, as if it's thinking things over.
23	A mirror of polished saltglass. In it, the Lantern's light looks like dawn. In the room, it looks like noon.
24	A cool bed, a cold supper, and a jug of real water? Sixty gold. The water alone is worth forty.
25	the Cool Well
26	The Hierophant's men used to ride through and bless the wells.
27	Now they ride through and _count_ them. Why would you count wells? Wells don't go anywhere.
28	...Unless you expect to need every one of them.
29	I'm writing a ballad about the Sunscar. So far it rhymes "sand" with "sand" eleven times.
30	Critics call it "accurate."
31	I used to run the salt road in four days. Now I walk to the well and back and call it a day's work.
32	Best days of my life, then and now. Only now's got Nim in it.
33	Papa came home! He was SO dirty. Mama made him take two baths.
34	I drew you a picture. It's you, and the big one, and the girl with black hair, and the little one with the Crest. The little one is the biggest because he asked.
