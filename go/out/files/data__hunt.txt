1	Old Gnarlhide
2	the East Fields of Thistledown
3	A boar the size of a hay cart has been tearing up the East Fields outside Thistledown. The farmers call him Old Gnarlhide and leave him the south field.
4	A boar the size of a hay cart, gray with age, rooting through the stubble. Its tusks are as long as Wren's arm. It lifts its head and looks straight at you.
5	Old Gnarlhide goes down like a felled oak, and the field is quiet.
6	The Wold Widow
7	the heart of the Whisperwold
8	In the Whisperwold's heart there's a web strung between two oaks, wide as a sail. Whatever spun it is called the Widow, and her brood comes with her.
9	A web between two oaks, wide as a sail, beaded with dew and worse. Something the color of a bruise sits at its center. It has far too many eyes, and all of them open.
10	The Widow curls up like a burnt leaf. Her web sags and tears in the wind.
11	Mother Knot
12	the sewers under Aurenne
13	The rat-catchers won't go into the deep sewers under Aurenne anymore. They say there's a rat king down there, a hundred rats knotted at the tail, and it eats its own young to heal.
14	A heap in the dark that squeaks with a hundred voices: rats knotted tail to tail into one great shuddering thing. The middle of it has a face.
15	Mother Knot comes apart into a hundred fleeing rats. None of them look back.
16	Glasseye
17	the deep dunes of the Sunscar
18	Pilgrims crossing the Sunscar talk of a basilisk with one eye of clear glass. Look into it and your legs turn to stone, they say. Look away and it breathes fire.
19	A long, low drake basks on a dune of fused sand. One of its eyes is clear glass with the sun burning in it. The air around it shimmers.
20	Glasseye's glass eye cracks across. Then the light goes out of it.
21	Old Belcher
22	the Drownmere in the Mirefen
23	In the Drownmere there's a toad as big as a cottage. The marsh-folk call him Old Belcher. He sings people to sleep, then sits on them.
24	What looked like a mossy hillock in the bog opens two yellow eyes. It is a toad, as big as a cottage. Its throat swells like a bellows.
25	Old Belcher deflates with one long, last croak that echoes across the whole mere.
26	Cinderjaw
27	the ash of the Drift
28	Out on the Drift there's a drake that swims through the ash like a fish through water. Salvagers call it Cinderjaw. The ash catches fire wherever it surfaces.
29	The ash heaves and something surfaces: a drake of black slag, embers glowing between its scales. Its jaws drip fire.
30	Cinderjaw sinks back into the ash and does not surface again. The embers go gray.
31	Hymnal
32	the Drowned Choir under ruined Aurenne
33	Under the ruins of Aurenne, where the Choir used to sing, an eel as long as a nave has learned the hymns. Those who hear it stop being able to speak.
34	In the flooded choir loft, an eel as long as the nave coils between the pews. When it opens its mouth, it sings in a choirboy's voice.
35	Hymnal's last note breaks off in the middle. The loft is silent for the first time in a year.
36	The Scrap King
37	the Boneyard below Corvin's Roost
38	In the Boneyard under Corvin's Roost, the scrap has crowned itself a king: a golem that bolts drones together out of junk, then eats them to mend itself.
39	A throne of wrecked engines, and on it a giant of scrap wearing a crown of bent rivets. Little drones buzz around it like flies. It stands up.
40	The Scrap King comes apart bolt by bolt. The crown rolls to your feet. It's only a cog.
41	Grandmother Frost
42	The monks of Frosthollow whisper about Grandmother Frost: a white shape on the pass that sings travelers to sleep in the snow, and the wolves come for them after.
43	A shape of falling snow in the shape of an old woman, humming. Wolves of white frost sit around her, like grandchildren listening to a story.
44	Grandmother Frost stops humming. The snow she was made of settles, softly, over nothing.
45	Neverwake
46	the Gloam Rift
47	Deep in the Gloam Rift there's an eye that has never closed. Whoever it looks at falls asleep, and some of them never wake.
48	An eye as tall as a door hangs in the dark of the Rift, unblinking. You feel very, very tired.
49	Neverwake shuts. For the first time, perhaps ever, it sleeps.
