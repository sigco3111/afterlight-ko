1	The tower's coming down! Everybody out — that includes you, Lord-Commander.
2	I'll walk. I'll not be carried out of my own Lantern by a squire.
3	Bram: Knight. Sir Bram Kestrel.
4	Garrick: Hm. And who dubbed you?
5	Bram: A deserter.
6	Garrick: Ha. Of course he did.
7	Bram: Then walk.
8	The Grand Lantern. Bram fought Garrick alone. He went down twice and got up twice. He didn't kill him. Garrick gave us the Shard and walked out on his own. Bram's hands didn't stop shaking for an hour. He says that's the trick.
9	Pressed between two volumes of liturgy: a child's drawing. Two stick figures under an enormous yellow sun. The tall one is labeled CASS. The small one is labeled ME.
10	Underneath, in a grown man's careful hand: _Lysa, age 7._
11	...She drew the sun too big.
12	Everybody draws the sun too big when they're seven.
13	A Choir ledger. Columns of names from the Undercroft, each with a date and a single word beside it: _Offered._ At the foot of every page, the same initials, in the same careful hand as the drawing: C.L.
14	He knew. About Mercy. About the Undercroft. He signed every page.
15	He dismissed her in front of the whole city.
16	He's always very kind in front of people.
17	The Halcyon moors at the end of the old dock. The dock hangs over nothing now.
18	This is where she stood. This is where she gave you Dad's compass, and told you to go.
19	...I came back. I'm late.
20	That was not her. That was the island remembering her.
