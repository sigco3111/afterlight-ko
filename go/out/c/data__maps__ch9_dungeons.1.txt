1	Winch
2	The Marigold. A sky-ferry. Forty seats, still bolted in, all facing the windows.
3	On one seat, a paper bag of sweets, unopened. Someone was saving them for the view.
4	You climb the crane-tower. From the top, the Boneyard is a river of broken ships, running all the way down the canyon to the dark.
5	Far to the north-east, three bright shards hang in a slanting line, as if someone had thrown them and they never landed.
6	Strongbox
7	The strongbox lies open and quiet. Whatever it was, it is only a box now.
8	A gilded strongbox, half-buried beside the upturned hull of the Gilded Gull. The lock is shaped like a smile.
9	Pry it open?
10	The smile gets wider.
11	The Gilded Gull, lying on her back like a beetle. Her lift-coil housing is empty, torn open from the inside.
12	Censer
13	Luggage, still strapped to the rail. A name-tag: TILDE, AGED 9, TO AURENNE. PLEASE RETURN IF LOST.
14	Nobody did.
15	A barrel of hymnals, swollen with rain into a single block of paper. The top one is open to "Rest Ye, Dawn-Bound."
16	A ward of crystallized light hums around the mainmast. Up close you can hear it: the hymn, over and over, the same eight bars.
17	Crow's nest
18	The crow's nest has snapped off and rolled to this islet. You climb into it. From up here, through the rain, the shards wheel slowly around a single point of light.
19	The point of light does not wheel. It sits in the middle of everything, perfectly still.
