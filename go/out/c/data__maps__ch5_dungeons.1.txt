2	A worn grave-slab: BRENNA HALLOWAY — FIRST OF US — SHE CUT THE STAIR.
3	ODA HALLOWAY. SHE HELD THE LIGHT THROUGH THE STORM OF THE SEVENTY BOATS, AND ALL SEVENTY CAME HOME.
4	IDE HALLOWAY. "SHE SANG FLAT AND NEVER ONCE MISSED A DAWN." Your grandmother. You remember her hands, and her terrible singing.

6	Gate

8	A blank slab, smoothed and ready. Nothing carved on it yet.
9	A small, newer stone, the letters cut in a careful, narrow hand you know very well: EDRIN — WHO WAS NOT A KEEPER, BUT KEPT US.
10	The first Keeper, worn faceless by four hundred years of damp. Fresh candle stubs at her feet. Someone comes down here often. Someone who comes down from the top.
11	The great lamp of Lanternfall. Its bed of brass is empty.
