15	You drop a pebble. Four heartbeats later, very faintly: _plink._

17	Master Ruel's furnace. The air above it wobbles like a mirage that can't make up its mind.
18	The Lamp
19	The lamp of Saltglass burns inside a cage of saltglass panes. The light comes out of the glass in every direction at once.
20	Up close it hums — low, like a cat that's pleased with itself. Or a cat that isn't.
