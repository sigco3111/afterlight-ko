21	I said goodbye to Mom. A real one, this time. She said a Keeper is someone who keeps a light going for people out in the dark. Then she let go, and the Lantern went out, and the light was in my hand. Grow up well, she said. I'm trying.
22	The Lantern log of Lanternfall, open on its stand, two hundred years of weather and oil in neat columns. One page is creased, as if it has been opened to many times.
23	_14th of Harvest. Keeper absent from the lamp, second bell to fourth. Keeper at sea in search of the fishing boat Gull's Return, lost with one aboard. — Y.H._
24	And below it, in a hand that is not quite steady: _Lamp relit at fourth bell. Wren asleep. Did not find him. Do not tell her._
25	...She went out. Keepers never leave the Lantern. She went out after him.
