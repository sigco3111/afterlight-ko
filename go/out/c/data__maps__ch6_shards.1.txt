1	Lamp
2	The cot you have slept in for a year. Someone has patched the blanket a dozen times, with whatever was to hand. One patch is a piece of a Dawnguard flag.
3	A pot of soup, gently burning. It smells of ash-potato and, very faintly, of fish.
4	Rock
5	A cairn of stacked gray stones. Scratched on the flat stone at its foot: EDRIN HALLOWAY. HE'D WANT A BETTER STONE.
6	A newer cairn. The stones are still sharp-edged. YSOLDE HALLOWAY. KEEPER. SHE HELD.
7	A little fishing boat, upright on the ash, a long way from any water. Painted on the bow: THE SEAGULL. Fennick's boat. It rose with the sea, and came down here, with him.
8	Fennick's garden. Ash-potatoes, a stubborn rosemary, and — tucked in a sheltered corner — one sprig of sea lavender, in a pot, carefully watered.
9	A practice post, hacked half through. Fennick's been keeping his arm in. Or keeping busy.
10	Not yet. Fennick wanted to show me something first.
11	The village well, torn up roots and all. WREN + ILSE + THE SEA + PIP. Somebody has added, in the last year, very small: COME HOME.
12	Look west
13	Table
14	Your bed, still made. Your mother made it the morning she wrote the letter. Nobody has slept in it since.
15	Shelf
16	The cutter's mast, snapped clean. A Dawnguard pennant still flutters from the stump, red and gold, very bright against the ash.
17	Lost Crest
18	Don't steal, don't fight, don't fall off. That's the whole law.
19	Folk keep falling off. You'd think it'd be the easy one.
20	Warden says you're going east. Alone.
