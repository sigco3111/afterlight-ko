21	Well
22	The well is frozen over. Someone has chipped a hole in the ice and left a ladle beside it, the way you'd leave a key under a mat.
23	Gate road's crawling with gray cloaks, sparrow. Wrong way.
24	The Ice Score
25	I keep airing the beds. Tolle would want them aired.
26	He wrote from Saltglass. Two pages about the children and one line about himself: "I am managing." He underlined "managing." That boy.
27	A guest book. Three winters back, in a big looping hand: "Sir Aldous Venn and squire. Couldn't sleep. Lovely tea."
28	Steps cut into the ice by hand, worn down in the middle by centuries of monks' feet. Each step has a number carved beside it. You are on step four hundred and twelve.
29	A little bell is carved into the wall by the Hearthstone, and under it: FOR THE ONES WHO HAD TO LEAVE IN A HURRY.
30	Boots on the stair behind us. There's no going back.
