24	I keep airing the beds. Tolle would want them aired.
25	He wrote from Saltglass. Two pages about the children and one line about himself: "I am managing." He underlined "managing." That boy.
26	A guest book. Three winters back, in a big looping hand: "Sir Aldous Venn and squire. Couldn't sleep. Lovely tea."
27	Steps cut into the ice by hand, worn down in the middle by centuries of monks' feet. Each step has a number carved beside it. You are on step four hundred and twelve.
28	A little bell is carved into the wall by the Hearthstone, and under it: FOR THE ONES WHO HAD TO LEAVE IN A HURRY.
29	Boots on the stair behind us. There's no going back.
