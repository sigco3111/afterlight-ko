41	Home. The east wall is gone; the sky looks in. Everything else is exactly where it was. Two plates on the table.
42	I left the table. I didn't even finish supper. That was the last supper we ever had, and I didn't finish it.
43	Outside, something is scratching at the door. Something that came up out of the sea on a stormy night, a long time ago, and never really left.
44	Two plates. She kept setting two plates. For seven years. Even after Dad.
45	No. Not for Dad. For me. One for her and one for me.
46	Your mother's herb garden. Rosemary gone to ash. Thyme gone to ash.
47	And the sea-lavender — the one that never flowered in all the years you knew it — is covered in small purple flowers.
48	...Now? Now you flower?
49	Perhaps it was waiting for someone to come home and see.
50	The lamp room of Lanternfall. The light is everywhere — in the walls, in the floor, in the air like warm water.
51	At the heart of it, with her back to you and her hands lifted to the lamp, a woman in a Keeper's robe. Her braid has come undone. The light pours out of her hands and does not stop.
52	Wren. You're late for supper.
53	Mom.
54	Come inside, little bird. It's getting dark. It's been getting dark for such a long time.
55	Mom, you can let go now. The boat got away. We made it. I made it. It's been a whole year.
56	If I let go, the dark comes in.
57	If I let go, you'll leave. You always wanted to leave. You used to toddle to the end of the dock and just _look_.
58	In Wren's pack, the little brass lantern her mother gave her flares, as if it has recognized someone.
59	Stay. I'll keep the light on. Nothing will take you. Not the sea. Not the Hierophant. Not _time_. You'll be sixteen forever, and I will never have to watch you go.
60	Wren. That is what he wants. That is Evernoon.
