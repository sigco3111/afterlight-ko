1	Carved inside the hollow oak, at a child's height: LYSA WAS HERE. Below it, in a careful, very old hand: SO WAS ELOWEN.
2	A cherry tree, still in blossom. The only living thing in the court. Petals fall and do not land; they hang in the air, as if waiting to be told what to do.
3	Lightning rod
4	A ward of raw lightning hangs across the bridge, humming like a hive. Your hair stands on end from three paces away.
5	A storm-rider of Stormcrown, carved in stone, arm flung up to the sky. The inscription: FLY INTO IT. Someone has added, underneath: BUT MAYBE NOT TODAY.
6	Another rider, younger, laughing. The sculptor caught a crack in the stone running right across the smile, and kept it.
7	The Stormcrown Pride, Vasha's flagship, broken across the rocks. Her figurehead is the Ardent herself, carved mid-shout, finger pointing at a horizon that isn't there.
8	Vasha's flight log, under a stone. Last entry: "Gave the Dawnstone to the Hierophant today. He held my hands and thanked me. I have never been so certain of anything." A week later, written so hard the pen tore the page: "WHAT HAVE I DONE."
9	Halt. ...No, I can't remember why I'm meant to say that either. Go on in. Everyone else is dead or upstairs.
10	Shelf
11	Ledger
12	The Bellwright Fountain. Dry for a year. Someone has dropped a single coin in it anyway, very recently, for luck.
13	A statue of the first Hierophant, arms raised to the sun. Someone has chalked a mustache on him. It is a very good mustache.
14	A Dawnguard cot, the blanket squared to regulation. Pinned above it: a child's drawing of a knight with a very large sword, signed TO DAD.
15	The cradle of the Great Lamp of Aurenne. The glass is cracked from rim to base. It was the brightest light in the world for nine hundred years.
16	Well
17	Garden
18	The village Hearthstone. Cold, for the first time in anyone's memory. Someone has left a bunch of dried sea-lavender on it.
19	Tobias's shack. A note pinned under a stone in his square capitals: BACK SOON. DON'T TOUCH THE NETS. The nets are still here. Nobody touched them.
20	Nana Brisa's house. Through the window, jars of herbs labeled in tiny exact handwriting. One just says: DO NOT. LET WREN. TOUCH.
