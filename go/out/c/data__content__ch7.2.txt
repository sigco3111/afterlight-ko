21	Drowned Acolyte
22	An acolyte of the Under-Choir. It drowned singing. It is still singing.
23	Knell Wisp
24	The last note of a bell that broke mid-toll. It drifts, still ringing, looking for the rest of itself.
25	Ossuary Warden
26	Aurenne's honored dead were buried with their swords. Some of them never put the swords down.
27	The Unfinished
28	One of Sister Mercy's Remembrances, never finished. It is trying very hard to remember a face. Any face.
29	Cistern Eel
30	They came up the drains when the sea fell into the sky. They are not going back.
31	Bellshell Crab
32	It wears a handbell for a shell. When it is angry, which is always, it rings.
33	Drowned Cantor
34	A cantor of the Under-Choir. Its voice holds the others together, the way a nail holds a coffin.
35	Drowned Chorister
36	One voice in the Drowned Choir. Alone, it is only a drowned child. Together, they are a hymn.
37	The Under-Choir's Precentor. He was told to hold the note until noon. He is a very obedient man.
38	Old Clapper
39	A rust-red wyrm that nested in the Reliquary and swallowed a bell whole. It tolls when it breathes.
40	The Held Note breaks over you — the Dawnsong, sung backward!
