42	"Louder. LOUDER. Let the water carry it!" The cistern surges.
43	"Hold it... hold it until noon..."
44	The Precentor lifts his baton. A drowned voice answers from the water.
45	The choristers hold one swelling note. Silence one of them — quickly!
46	_BONNNG._ The sound comes out as fire.
47	Old Clapper thrashes. Rust and bell-metal rain from the ceiling!
48	Deep in Old Clapper's belly, something begins to toll. Brace yourselves!
49	The City of Bells, silent and drifting. Ilse's Ember Ward keeps its people breathing.
