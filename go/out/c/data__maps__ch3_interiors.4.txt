61	A tuning anvil. Forty years of bells were tuned on it. There is a dent in it shaped like a word you can't quite read.
62	A ledger of every noon since Tolliver began. Every entry reads RANG TRUE — except seven that read FLAT — and then, today, in large shaky letters: RANG TRUE!!!
63	A ledger of every noon since Tolliver began. Every entry reads RANG TRUE. Except the last seven, which read FLAT. FLAT. FLAT. FLAT. FLAT. FLAT. FLAT.
64	A suit of Dawnguard plate on a stand, polished to a mirror. There is a notch in the breastplate, over the heart, that has never been hammered out.
65	A portrait: a broad, laughing knight with a ridiculous mustache, and a young woman trying very hard not to laugh with him.
66	Mm. Warm. So warm.
67	They said I'd never be cold again.
68	Sister Mercy says if I'm good, I can go downstairs where it's warm.
69	I'm being SO good.
70	The Sisters stay under watch until the Lord-Commander says otherwise.
71	They keep offering me soup. I keep saying no. It's the longest conversation I've ever had.
72	The almshouse ledger, in a neat round hand. ADMITTED: forty-one. DISCHARGED: none. Beside each name, a small mark like a closed eye.
73	Leek and barley soup. It smells wonderful. Under the wonderful, faintly, something sweet and sleepy.
74	Poppy-milk. Enough to make a grown man very, very agreeable. Nobody eat the soup.
