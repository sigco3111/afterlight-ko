61	Wren. That is what he wants. That is Evernoon.
62	...She's not wrong, though. That's the worst part. Some days it's what I'd want too.
63	But it isn't her. My mom never once asked me to stay. Not even at the dock. She wanted to, I _saw_ her want to. She gave me Dad's compass and she let me go.
64	That's who you are, Mom. Not this. I'm going to get you back to who you are.
65	Then I will make you stay.
66	The light gutters. The shadow that was wound around her peels away like wet paper, and burns, and is gone.
67	What is left is a tired woman kneeling on a stone floor, holding a lamp that is still, somehow, alight.
68	...Wren?
69	Oh. Look at you. You've grown. You— your _hair_. You cut it yourself, didn't you. It's terrible.
70	_(laughing, and not laughing)_ Ilse did the back.

72	She never would.
73	How long?
74	A year. I meant to hold it for a minute. Just until the boat was clear.
75	Something took hold of me afterwards. It kept saying, _not yet. Not yet. Keep her safe._ I think it was me. I'm sorry. I think it was only ever me.
76	I'm sorry. I'm sorry I took the Dawnstone. I'm sorry about what I said at supper. I'm sorry I left. I'm sorry I didn't come home sooner—
77	Wren. Little bird. Breathe.

79	You went out after Dad. That night. I found your hand-lamp, tied to the Gull's Return.
