23	Oswin
24	The Hierophant's Page! Going south? You'll want boots, water, and something sharp. I sell two of those.
25	Bellwright Arms & Sundries. Swords, salves, and the finest bell-bronze buckles in Aurenne.
26	A bell-founder's anvil, cracked clean through. Oswin keeps it "for sentiment." There is a price tag on it anyway.
27	Ledgers going back three generations of Bellwrights. The earliest entry: ONE (1) BELL, LARGE. PAID IN GEESE.
28	Imre
29	His Holiness is waiting for you in his study, Page. The left-hand door.
30	The Hierophant's study is through the left-hand door at the back. The archives, the right.
31	Brother Quill keeps the archives. He is older than some of the books and fonder of them than of people.
32	Welcome, pilgrim. The dawn-office is long over, but the light is always open.
33	I pray for my son every day. He's a sailor.
34	The Choir says the sea belongs to the Gloam after dark. So I pray very loudly at dusk.
35	Shh. I'm listening for the Dawnsong. You can hear it in here, if you're quiet.
36	...You're not being quiet.
37	Sister Mercy's name has been struck from the rolls. I struck it myself. My hand would not stop shaking.
38	Forty-one admitted. None discharged. How did none of us ever do the arithmetic?
39	One Crest, one life, one light. It is carved under St. Aurel's feet. We say it every dawn.
40	I have said it eleven thousand times. I counted. That is my Crest, I think: Counter.
