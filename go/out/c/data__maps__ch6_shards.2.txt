21	I'll keep the soup warm. Hale says I'm not to say that. I said it anyway.
22	East gate's shut. Strait's shut. Everything east of here's shut.
23	Warden Hale's orders. Ask at the big hull, north side.
24	Salvage and sundries! Everything I sell fell out of the sky, so it's all slightly dented.
25	Some of it's even better than new. The sky's full of dead rich men's luggage.
26	Seven wounds in the sky, child. I count them every hour. Somebody has to.
27	Six of them bleed white. One bleeds gold — far to the west, low down, near where Lanternfall was.
28	Somebody over there is still holding on to something. Holding very, very hard.
29	Twenty years a sailor out of Port Gale. Now look up. No — UP. See that shimmer, past the wounds?
30	That's the sea. All of it. Hanging up there, upside down, fish and all.
31	Now and then a fish falls out. Free dinner, if it doesn't land on you.
32	Ash-potatoes. They grow in anything. Taste of nothing. Keep you alive.
33	Like most things now, I suppose.
34	We're playing Lost Wall! Kay writes letters and I deliver them. It's way better than the other game.
35	We're playing Catch the Keeper's Girl! You be her! You have to run, and if we catch you, we throw you off the edge!
36	...PRETEND off the edge. Kay always cries if it's real off the edge.
37	I don't like that game. I think the Keeper's girl was probably just sad.
38	Mama says sad people do stupid things. Mama did one once. She married Papa.
39	Trader, out of Kiteholm. North, past the wind-stacks. Town of kites and scrap.
40	A gang of orphans runs the scrap-yards there. Boss is a boy — twelve, maybe. Mouth like a sailor, fingers like a magpie. He took my purse, then gave it back because I "looked like I needed it more."
