21	Old Belcher
22	the Drownmere in the Mirefen
23	In the Drownmere there's a toad as big as a cottage. The marsh-folk call him Old Belcher. He sings people to sleep, then sits on them.
24	What looked like a mossy hillock in the bog opens two yellow eyes. It is a toad, as big as a cottage. Its throat swells like a bellows.
25	Old Belcher deflates with one long, last croak that echoes across the whole mere.
26	Cinderjaw
27	the ash of the Drift
28	Out on the Drift there's a drake that swims through the ash like a fish through water. Salvagers call it Cinderjaw. The ash catches fire wherever it surfaces.
29	The ash heaves and something surfaces: a drake of black slag, embers glowing between its scales. Its jaws drip fire.
30	Cinderjaw sinks back into the ash and does not surface again. The embers go gray.
31	Hymnal
32	the Drowned Choir under ruined Aurenne
33	Under the ruins of Aurenne, where the Choir used to sing, an eel as long as a nave has learned the hymns. Those who hear it stop being able to speak.
34	In the flooded choir loft, an eel as long as the nave coils between the pews. When it opens its mouth, it sings in a choirboy's voice.
35	Hymnal's last note breaks off in the middle. The loft is silent for the first time in a year.
36	The Scrap King
37	the Boneyard below Corvin's Roost
38	In the Boneyard under Corvin's Roost, the scrap has crowned itself a king: a golem that bolts drones together out of junk, then eats them to mend itself.
39	A throne of wrecked engines, and on it a giant of scrap wearing a crown of bent rivets. Little drones buzz around it like flies. It stands up.
40	The Scrap King comes apart bolt by bolt. The crown rolls to your feet. It's only a cog.
