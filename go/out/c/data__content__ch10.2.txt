21	Time thickens like honey around you.
22	"NOON." Every bell in Evernoon rings at once.
23	"Sing with me, children."
24	Mercy lets down her hood. "You will not take her. You will not take ANY of them." She sings the Vespersong.
25	A single soft bell begins to toll, counting someone down...
26	"Hush. Be still. Be still forever."
27	"Rest now." A lullaby — and under it, the bells of Evernoon begin to swing...
28	"—IT!" A hundred mouths finish the sentence at once.
29	It begins to forget its own shape. Light pours through the gaps — light no longer heals it!
30	A puppet tears loose from its body and staggers forward!
