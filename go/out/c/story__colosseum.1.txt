1	III
2	Round one! From the kennels under the east stand — the SAND HOUNDS! They have not been fed. That is a joke. Mostly.
3	Round two! Brothers in arms, brothers in debt — KELL THE NET and KELL THE FIST!
4	The final round! He has eaten three gates and a herald — GRIZZLEMAW!
5	Round one! Imported at great expense from the Sunscar — the GLASS SCORPIONS! Do not step on them. They hate that.
6	Round two! Fastest blade in the Heartland, slowest to pay his tab — SERENO THE SWIFT!
7	The final round! Forged in the bell foundries from a broken bell and a bad idea — THE IRON BULL!
8	Round one! Dredged from the Fens at tremendous personal risk — the MIRE LURKERS!
9	Round two! One prays, one curses, neither has ever lost — the MASKS OF DAWN AND DUSK!
10	The final round! Mother of a thousand, mercy for none — THE SCYTHE MATRON!
11	Round one! They ride the bells' own echoes — the STORM KITES!
12	Round two! Three shields, one wall, no gaps — the PHALANX OF AURENNE!
13	The championship! Forty-one bouts. Forty-one wins. Your champion, and mine — VALKA THE UNDEFEATED!
