1	The Drowned Cantor
2	A Tidecaller who sang the boats home for forty years, and went down with the last one. She still keeps the crest-glass. She still sings.
3	The Bronze Abbot
4	The bell the founders were made to melt down, poured instead into a guardian and walled up with their calling. It has been waiting to be rung.
5	The Scrap Colossus
6	The first Kitewrights built it to lift their workshop into the sky. It never flew. It has guarded what they left ever since, out of loyalty or spite.
7	The Forgetting
8	What the Choir set to guard the last Mnemonist's grave: an appetite for memory. It eats names first.
9	The Drowned Cantor's hymn breaks over you like a wave!
10	She hums the song mothers sang on the docks. Your eyes grow heavy...
11	The Drowned Cantor draws a breath that sounds like the tide going out... Guard!
12	BONG. The floor of the foundry jumps.
13	The Abbot's bronze rings, and goes still and hard.
14	The Bronze Abbot swings back, back, back on its yoke... Guard!
15	The Colossus vents its whole boiler over you!
16	Plates fall away. What is left of it moves faster.
17	The Scrap Colossus whines higher and higher, steam screaming from every seam... Guard!
18	The Forgetting opens, and every name you know goes quiet for a moment.
19	Somewhere a clock forgets how to tick.
20	The Forgetting turns toward you, and you cannot remember why you came... Guard!
